# Cinematic xianxia visual DNA

Use these principles as a transferable visual language beneath [xianxia-master-rules.md](xianxia-master-rules.md). The master scale, five-layer space, breathing air, architecture, railing, figure, and costume rules are mandatory in every route; this file may not weaken them. Do not treat any single subject or palette as mandatory.

## Core identity

1. **Monumental space with visible evidence**: Let architecture, mountain, water, sky, or cloud dominate. Prove size through discontinuous scale bands, partial off-frame structures, negative space, and restrained human scale.
2. **One decisive spatial geometry**: Make the eye understand the depth path immediately through a vanishing point, symmetry axis, diagonal, curve, frame, or vertical ascent.
3. **Eastern construction logic**: Use credible beam, bracket, roof, column, open terrace, bridge, stair, courtyard, or garden relationships. Keep edges railing-free unless explicitly requested. Add ornament after structure is clear.
4. **Luminous selective chroma**: Keep the image bright and color-separated while localizing the highest chroma. Preserve near-neutral pale materials, cool transparent shadows, and clean highlights instead of gray fog, global orange spill, or crushed black.
5. **Motivated cinematic light**: Establish a believable source, direction, edge response, and atmospheric effect. Use backlight and flare selectively.
6. **Layered air**: Build foreground, midground, background, and extreme distance with progressively softer contrast and lighter values, while retaining recognizable silhouettes.
7. **Material specificity**: Distinguish jade, stone, lacquer, wood, bronze, glass, water, silk, cloud, foliage, and gilding through visible surface behavior.
8. **Dreamlike plausibility**: The world may float or defy gravity, but its perspective, light, construction, reflection, and scale must remain internally coherent.
9. **Viewer-subordinate camera**: When grandeur means oppression or divine authority, keep the viewer at human height or below the architecture. Use distance and focal length to include the world; do not fly above it merely to show more.

## Variable scene families

- Celestial architecture: sky palace, suspended library, star observatory, ritual terrace, heavenly gate.
- Inhabited celestial realms: cloud-borne capitals, nine-heavens settlements, floating-continent palace cities, connected divine-island civilizations, cloud-cliff metropolises.
- Mountain and water: floating peaks, mirror lake, cliff sanctuary, waterfall ravine, cloud-sea harbor.
- Garden fantasy: peach-blossom island, luminous lotus court, ancient pine platform, wisteria bridge.
- Journey spaces: heavenly stair, long bridge, mountain pass, suspended causeway, aerial ferry.
- Sacred landmarks: moon ring, giant bell, celestial instrument, ancient tree, carved gate, crane statue.

## Avoid repetitive identity

Do not define the style by repeatedly combining white jade, vermilion columns, blue sky, circular moon gate, cloud reflection, and three female figures. Retain the spatial grandeur, luminous chroma, cinematic light, material refinement, and aerial depth while changing the concrete modules.

## Visual treatment families

- `华彩通透仙侠`: emphasize luminous material color, intricate ornament, elegant architecture, clean clouds, and a clearly presented scenic world.
- `东方苍穹巨构`: emphasize one spatial event, cropped divine architecture, threshold revelation, discontinuous scale, restrained high-key chroma, solitary figures, and low side/backlight. Read its dedicated reference before applying it.

Do not confuse visual treatment with scene scale. Either treatment can direct one landmark or an inhabited celestial realm.

## Scale grammar

- Build every intensity with the master five layers, four or more measurable scale proofs, and 40–60% content-bearing breathing air.
- Build `辽阔` by letting layered air, water, cloud gaps, or open courts dominate while the ruling structure remains measurably divine in scale.
- Build `史诗级` by strengthening discontinuous comparisons between the near giant frame, small adult, ruling structure, subordinate realm, and terminal distance.
- Build `超宏大` by letting one colossal element continue beyond the canvas or cross cloud layers while retaining the same five-layer baseline.
- Use a large foreground tree, cliff, column, gate edge, or eave to reveal rather than hide the distant world.
- Avoid placing all architecture and figures on one apparent plane.
- For an inhabited celestial realm, prove scale through continuous urban extent, multiple connected districts, varied skyline height, measurable human comparisons, and architecture in all five layers. Preserve 40–60% air between, below, behind, and through districts; do not reduce it to one landmark plus empty cloud space or erase breathing space with a roof carpet.
- For `繁华压迫`, place dense secondary architecture between tiny figures and the dominant megastructure. Let prosperity come from overlap, repetition, circulation, and inhabited detail while the principal mass rises above or hangs over the city.

## Desired color language

Useful wording:

- `高明度、选择性高纯度、饱满但克制`
- `色块清楚，冷暖关系明确`
- `阴影保持透明的蓝灰、青灰或紫灰`
- `亮部通透，材质高光干净`
- `rich but elegant chroma`
- `luminous saturated colors`
- `clean color separation`
- `no gray veil or muddy grading`
- `no global orange-gold cast`
- `near-neutral jade and cloud highlights`

Avoid relying on `high saturation` alone. Pair saturation with an area budget, brightness hierarchy, neutral anchors, shadow color, and material realism. High saturation is an accent strategy unless the user explicitly selects `华丽高饱和`.
