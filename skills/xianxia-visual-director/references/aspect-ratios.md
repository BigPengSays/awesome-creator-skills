# Aspect-ratio composition routes

Apply exactly one route beneath [xianxia-master-rules.md](xianxia-master-rules.md). The ratio changes framing behavior, not the inherited five layers, 40–60% content-bearing breathing air, measurable scale, railing-free architecture, or figure and costume rules.

## 16:9 — default cinematic landscape

- Use for balanced establishing shots, architecture plus landscape, and general cinematic scenes.
- Spread the fixed five layers across a balanced cinematic width and favor an off-center ruling subject near a thirds intersection.
- Allow a strong diagonal, one-point corridor, centered axis, or framed vista.
- Keep environment-led scale-marker figures around 1–4% of frame height; use 5–8% only when the full silhouette must read, and never enlarge them merely because the frame is wide.
- Preserve breathing room for sky, cloud volume, reflections, or distant islands.
- For celestial-city oppression, keep the horizon near the lower third, use a distant low or eye-level camera, and let the principal mass dominate the middle and upper frame. A wide canvas does not imply an aerial view.

Prompt cue: `16:9 横向电影画幅，宽阔建立镜头，五层空间与有内容空气清晰`.

## 21:9 — panoramic spectacle

- Use for immense cloud seas, floating-island chains, long bridges, processions, and lateral architecture.
- Spread environment storytelling horizontally while keeping one dominant anchor.
- Use foreground elements at one or both edges to prevent an empty strip-like frame.
- Keep vertical landmarks away from being cropped; reduce their relative height.
- Use layered lateral rhythm rather than filling the center with many objects.
- For palace density and pressure, favor low-viewpoint telephoto compression across the width; do not turn the panorama into a top-down city map.

Prompt cue: `21:9 超宽银幕画幅，横向展开的史诗尺度，边缘框景与远景层叠`.

## 4:3 — classical tableau

- Use for formal architecture, courtyards, pavilions, symmetrical gates, and storybook-like cinematic tableaux.
- Favor centered symmetry, stable triangles, or slightly off-center one-point perspective.
- Allocate more vertical space than 16:9 for roofs, clouds, trees, and reflections.
- Keep the composition stately and dense without crowding the corners.

Prompt cue: `4:3 古典横幅画幅，稳定庄严的空间组织，建筑与天空比例均衡`.

## 3:2 — photographic film still

- Use for naturalistic cinematic stills, diagonal movement, travel moments, and environment-led character scenes.
- Favor asymmetrical thirds, leading lines, believable lens placement, and less formal staging.
- Keep foreground-to-midground transitions natural and avoid excessively perfect symmetry unless requested.

Prompt cue: `3:2 摄影横幅画幅，自然电影剧照构图，非对称三分法与明确引导线`.

## 4:5 — editorial portrait canvas

- Use for tall gates, pavilion facades, waterfalls, ancient trees, fashion-led environment scenes, and poster-like compositions.
- Stack foreground, subject, anchor, and sky vertically.
- Keep important architecture away from side crops; use a narrower path or staircase as the depth axis.
- If people remain scale markers, place them in the lower third and keep the landmark dominant.
- Reserve controlled space above the anchor for cloud, canopy, or light shafts.

Prompt cue: `4:5 竖幅画面，纵向层叠构图，下方人物尺度参照，上方建筑与天光主导`.

## 9:16 — immersive vertical spectacle

- Use for soaring towers, heavenly stairs, cliff waterfalls, giant trees, deep chasms, and mobile-first visuals.
- Build an explicit bottom-to-top reading path. Use vertical repetition and a high or low vanishing point.
- Avoid shrinking a horizontal panorama into the narrow canvas.
- Keep the primary landmark inside the central vertical corridor and use side architecture as framing.
- Limit horizontal island chains and broad group arrangements; prioritize height and ascent.

Prompt cue: `9:16 沉浸式竖屏画幅，自下而上的视觉路径，强烈高度感与纵向空间层叠`.

## Ratio audit

Before finalizing, verify:

- The prompt names one supported ratio in its first sentence.
- The composition follows that ratio's reading direction.
- The focal anchor is not likely to be cropped.
- Figures remain at the intended scale.
- Negative space is purposeful rather than accidental.
- All five master layers remain readable and 40–60% content-bearing air survives the crop.
- Edges remain railing-free and any people retain the master rear-facing costume grammar.
- Camera height matches the emotional intent; `大全景` and a wide ratio have not been misread as permission for a high-angle view.

## Interaction with scale intensity

- Apply ratio first, then scale intensity. Do not change the requested canvas to obtain grandeur.
- Apply camera intent independently from ratio. No supported ratio defaults to bird's-eye or elevated framing.
- For every intensity, retain all five master layers and at least four measurable scale proofs.
- For `史诗级`, strengthen the ruling subject and discontinuous comparisons without deleting breathing air.
- For `超宏大`, add off-frame or cross-cloud continuation even in 4:5 or 9:16 by stacking the five layers vertically.
- In 21:9, use lateral extreme-distance layers and edge framing; do not scatter many equally large landmarks across the width.
- In 4:5 and 9:16, use lower-third tiny figures, a vertically dominant off-frame structure, and a high or low extreme-distance destination.

## Interaction with `仙界大境原典`

- Preserve the inherited fixed five layers and 40–60% content-bearing breathing air in every supported ratio; the canon route adds stricter cloud, anchor, motion, and light controls.
- Keep the principal cloud sea below the figure-bearing platform and mainly within the bottom 20–35%, adapting its shape rather than moving it above the architecture.
- Use the ratio only to change reading direction, crop, and placement. Do not change the route's measurable scale, limited far anchors, railing-free architecture, single primary motion, or warm diffuse-light baseline.
- Treat `9:16` as one vertical image by default. Never infer nine images, a sequence, a collage, or a figure-distribution schedule from the ratio.
