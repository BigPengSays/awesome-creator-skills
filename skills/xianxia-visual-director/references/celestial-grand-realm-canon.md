# Celestial grand-realm canon route

Use [xianxia-master-rules.md](xianxia-master-rules.md) first. Apply `仙界大境原典` as a sealed, independent creative route that inherits the shared master scale, five layers, breathing air, architecture, railing, figure, and costume rules, then adds stricter cloud, far-anchor, motion, and warm-light controls. It is not a nine-image series mode and never forces `9:16`.

## Route authority

- Select this route only when the user explicitly says `仙界大境原典`, `仙界总则`, `仙界原典`, or asks to follow the supplied heavenly-realm canon.
- When selected, apply this file after the shared master rules and before all normal scene, visual-style, camera-intent, density, color, and atmosphere defaults. Do not silently blend in `单体仙境`, `神域聚居地`, `华彩通透仙侠`, or `东方苍穹巨构` additions.
- Use the aspect-ratio reference only to adapt reading direction. Use the general composition library only for technical execution that does not conflict with this canon.
- Let explicit user additions extend the route only when they preserve its hard invariants. If an explicit request conflicts, name the conflict instead of quietly replacing the canon.

## World promise

Preserve this reading order and mood:

`first: immeasurable heavenly architecture -> second: a complete distant celestial realm -> third: a small back-facing immortal -> fourth: a continuing path into unseen heaven`

Keep the world `空而不寂，满而不塞，巨而不乱，动而不躁，华丽而克制，通透而安静`.

## Inherited hard scale system

- Make a principal column diameter equal to roughly five to ten adult heights and its complete height equal to roughly forty to eighty adult heights.
- Make a giant opening, jade screen, portal, or structural aperture equal to roughly twenty to fifty adult heights.
- Let a principal eave span hundreds of body widths and continue beyond the frame.
- Keep human-usable stair risers and bridge surfaces, but let the full stair, bridge, gallery, roofline, or platform cross cloud layers and converge toward an apparently inexhaustible distance.
- Include at least four visible scale proofs in every prompt: an off-frame giant fragment; adult-to-column, portal, screen, or plinth comparison; repeated elements converging into depth; ten to thirty body lengths of open space around the figure; vertically separated palaces; a cloud-sea crossing; near-and-far giant forms; or a distant giant structure shown only in part.

Do not replace these relationships with adjectives such as `宏大` or `史诗` alone.

## Inherited fixed five-layer space

Build all five layers even when the ratio changes:

1. A near-camera giant frame: column, eave, screen, pine trunk, cliff, portal edge, or other massive fragment.
2. A broad human-bearing surface: platform, stair, gallery, mirror ground, shallow-water ground, or its open edge.
3. One ruling heavenly megastructure.
4. One to three lighter groups of remote palaces, bridges, platforms, or architectural fragments.
5. A terminal celestial distance: giant moon, low sky light, heavenly river, galaxy, low cloud sea, or boundless clear sky.

- Preserve roughly 40–60% breathing space. Treat it as layered celestial air with value change and distant silhouettes, not blank sky.
- Interleave solid mass and open air. Let sky enter portals, column fields, screens, corridors, and beneath eaves.
- Keep near forms heavier, sharper, darker, and more detailed; make distance lighter, softer, lower-contrast, and more generalized without turning it gray.

## Low cloud sea and local mist

- Keep the main cloud sea below the architectural platform level. Its top must remain below the figure's feet and platform edge.
- Let the cloud sea occupy mainly the bottom 20–35% of the image, with rounded, thick, clustered cumulus volume and a stable low horizon.
- Keep people, platforms, plinths, columns, corridors, screens, eaves, and roofs fully above the main cloud layer.
- Keep the upper sky and the air around figures clear and transparent.
- Distinguish local fairy mist from the cloud sea. Allow only thin, translucent, nearly colorless mist beneath foundations, beside waterfalls, or between selected distance layers. Never wrap the whole city in fog.

## Controlled celestial content

Give each image exactly:

- one ruling megastructure;
- one far-realm anchor;
- one primary dynamic phenomenon or quiet sign of life;
- one near scale reference;
- one or a few immortals.

Select one or two compatible far relationships, not a catalog: remote giant palace fragments, an extremely long sky bridge, receding halls along the cloud edge, a lit gate silhouette, a transparent waterfall, an arc wall, a heavenly river, one crane before a moon, or separated floating platforms.

Use only one primary motion system: a cloud waterfall, transparent water fall, heavenly river, shallow-water ripple, wind-driven banner or veil, one crane, a high-altitude water channel, or thin cloud flow beneath a platform. Make it slow, broad, gravity-aware, and subordinate to architectural stillness.

## Optional world modules

- **Giant moon**: optional per image. When selected, use a realistic lunar surface, maria, craters, and terminator; occupy about 25–50% of frame width; place it low, off-center, partially cropped, or divided by architecture.
- **Ancient welcome pine**: optional, never automatic. Use a trunk diameter of roughly one to three adult heights and a crown spanning twenty to forty body widths. Let an off-frame trunk or long branch frame rather than decorate the scene.
- **Ground**: choose one coherent type: a thin transparent water film with restrained ripples; an immense edgeless mirror platform; shallow-relief white-stone stairs; a polished suspended platform; a curved shallow waterway ending in a fall; or a broad empty jade plane.

## Heavenly architecture

- Use Chinese spatial order without reconstructing a specific dynasty.
- Recompose extended eaves, colossal column fields, moon openings, giant stone screens, corridors, heavenly stairs, layered roofs, cloud platforms, and suspended gates into a unique celestial language.
- Keep architecture immense, simple, quiet, and structurally legible: large uninterrupted material planes, few organizing lines, repeated components for distance, and broad plain surfaces for weight.
- Concentrate decoration in one local zone. Use large shallow reliefs derived from cloud flow, water movement, star tracks, moon halos, wind marks, or celestial arcs; keep the remaining surfaces calm.
- Favor heavy pale stone, pearl-gray stone, restrained vermilion or ochre timber at roofs and load-bearing tops, narrow pale-gold ridge lines, and selective mineral religious color near upper structures.
- Use no railing of any kind on ground, bridges, platforms, stairs, or architecture unless the user explicitly overrides this canon.

## Immortals and costume

- Default to one fictional adult or a very small group, seen from the full back, 30–60 degree rear angle, side-rear walking view, or over-shoulder rear view.
- Default environment-led figures to 1–4% of frame height. Use 5–8% when the full immortal silhouette must read. Use 12–20% only when the user explicitly asks for an over-shoulder or costume-led frame and preserve scale through off-frame architecture and a distant heavenly realm.
- Use calm autonomous actions: slow walking, distant watching, standing at an open platform edge, ascending stairs, entering a curved gallery, relaxed hands, or lightly gathering one sleeve.
- Keep body and hair stable while all cloth responds to one high-altitude wind direction.
- Follow `衣有骨、纱有气、饰有止、色有主`: two resilient wind-catching lengths extend from shoulder, arm, or waist; two to four unequal rear hems lag behind movement; a floor-length robe and one vivid trailing outer cape may supply the controlled color accent.

## Light, color, and realism

- Light the realm with broad, soft, transparent high-altitude diffusion. Keep the low sun near the horizon or outside the frame so it supplies only gentle direction, warm yellow-gold temperature, and a very thin rim.
- Let the sky act as a giant soft source across stone, jade, timber, roof tile, water, cloth, pine, moon, and cloud sea. Keep highlights warm and delicate and shadows bright, open, and transparent.
- Maintain an overall warm late-afternoon feeling without applying one uniform orange filter. Preserve material separation in pale stone, pearl gray, vermilion or ochre timber, pale gold, clear sky, and garment accents.
- Use large-format architectural-photography and practical film-set realism: stone weight, warm jade translucency, clear timber and roof construction, real bark, water surface tension, continuous water and cloud flow, cumulus volume, cloth gravity and translucency, realistic lunar detail, and credible human proportion.

## Ratio adaptation

- For `16:9` and `21:9`, extend eaves, galleries, cloud edges, and far anchors laterally while retaining all five layers.
- For `4:3` and `3:2`, balance the ruling structure with a broad platform and a clear terminal distance.
- For `4:5` and `9:16`, stack the five layers vertically and use stairs, columns, waterfalls, moon arcs, or high openings to maintain the upward reading path.
- Never infer a series, collage, or multi-image output from `9:16`. One request produces one prompt unless the user explicitly asks for several.
- Default to a low or human-height camera. Use a high-angle or aerial view only when explicitly requested; never use it merely to show more architecture.

## Prompt assembly and audit

Assemble as:

`ratio -> camera height, distance, tilt, focal length -> ruling structure -> four or more scale proofs -> fixed five layers -> 40–60% breathing air -> one far anchor -> low cloud sea -> one primary motion -> architecture and ground without railings -> immortal and costume -> broad warm diffuse light -> physical materials -> invariants and exclusions`

Before output, verify every answer is yes:

- Is the route explicitly labeled `仙界大境原典` and kept independent?
- Is the requested ratio preserved without any nine-image assumption?
- Are four or more visible scale proofs present with measurable human comparisons?
- Are all five layers assigned and readable?
- Is 40–60% breathing space filled with layered air rather than blankness?
- Is the main cloud sea below the platform and mainly in the bottom 20–35%?
- Are distant palaces limited, lighter, softer, and non-urban in density?
- Is there exactly one ruling structure, one far anchor, and one primary motion or life sign?
- Are all railings absent?
- Are the figure scale, rear-facing angle, costume, and wind direction explicit?
- Is light broad, warm, soft, and transparent rather than hard, gray, or globally orange?
- Do materials and perspective remain physically credible?

If any answer is no, revise before responding. Do not substitute a generic palace city, dense roof carpet, isolated pavilion, gray fog panorama, hard gold backlight, overdecorated historical palace, or automatic megastructure-pressure composition.
