# Eastern sky megastructure style

Use `东方苍穹巨构` as a visual treatment, not a scene category. Apply [xianxia-master-rules.md](xianxia-master-rules.md) first, then add cropping, threshold revelation, and discontinuous scale without weakening the inherited five layers, 40–60% content-bearing air, monumental Eastern language, railing-free edges, or immortal costume. Apply it to either `单体仙境` or `神域聚居地`.

## Master inheritance

- Map the fixed five layers to: near giant frame or threshold; human-bearing surface with tiny rear-facing immortals; cropped ruling megastructure event; lighter subordinate realm; terminal sky, moon, cloud sea, higher heaven, or extreme-distance settlement.
- Include four or more measurable scale proofs. Cropping alone does not prove size; add adult-to-column, opening, bridge, platform, wall, tree, cliff, or distant-realm comparisons.
- Preserve 40–60% content-bearing air inside and around the event through portals, open courts, void beneath foundations, cloud gaps, water, sky, and distance. Do not confuse a solid gray field with breathing space.
- Keep all thresholds, platforms, bridges, stairs, galleries, and architectural edges railing-free unless explicitly requested.
- When people are present, keep them around 1–4%, rear-facing or rear-oblique, in calm action, with structured immortal clothing and one wind direction.

## Core treatment

- Build one dominant **spatial event**, not one fully visible landmark. Examples: a city beneath an overhanging heavenly gate, a bridge entering an unreachable palace, a waterfall splitting a cloud metropolis, or a divine realm revealed through a circular threshold.
- Show the megastructure as a fragment, underside, wall, gate opening, immense platform, cropped roof mass, vertical foundation, or distant architectural horizon. Continue it beyond at least one frame edge.
- Use deliberate obstruction and threshold viewing. Let eaves, portals, cliff faces, pines, columns, open dark platforms, or cloud gaps reveal the world.
- Make scale discontinuous across the inherited five layers: near giant frame -> human-bearing threshold and tiny adult -> ruling colossal event -> subordinate realm -> terminal distance.
- Preserve internal physical credibility while allowing impossible suspension, inverted weight, cloud-borne foundations, sky waterfalls, and architecture that replaces the horizon.

## Composition library

Choose one dominant event geometry; vary it across derivatives.

- **Sky-pressure overhang**: a cropped gate, palace underside, or enormous platform occupies the upper frame while a city or cloud sea lies below.
- **Threshold revelation**: a shaded doorway, open terrace, cliff edge, or column field opens onto a much brighter unreachable realm.
- **Aperture within the frame**: a moon ring, gate opening, arch, paired eaves, or pine-and-cliff gap encloses the distant subject.
- **Bridge into the unknowable**: a narrow bridge or cloud stair begins near tiny figures and disappears into cloud, light, or the base of a colossal palace.
- **Vertical rupture**: a waterfall, light shaft, hanging foundation, or sky river cuts through city, cloud, and void.
- **Undercroft city reveal**: an enormous upper structure floats over a carpet of subordinate roofs and courts.
- **Architectural section**: use a close or medium view of a giant wall, carved slab, foundation, column field, or partially visible roof system to imply a larger whole.

Do not reuse the moon ring or centered bridge automatically. Select the geometry from the scene promise.

## Scale and framing gate

For epic or ultra-macro output, require:

1. One main structure cropped, occluded, or continued outside the frame.
2. Adult scale markers commonly around 1–3% of frame height; use 5–8% only when an explicitly requested full silhouette must read, and preserve rear angle and costume grammar.
3. All five inherited layers and at least four measurable scale proofs, including one direct impossible comparison.
4. A railing-free foreground threshold or human-bearing surface that reveals rather than blocks the principal event.
5. A subordinate realm and readable terminal distance instead of empty fog.
6. Roughly 40–60% content-bearing cloud, void, sky, water, opening, court, or distance that exposes suspension and height without shrinking the architecture into an isolated object.

The megastructure may occupy roughly one-third to two-thirds of the frame, but do not use a fixed percentage when a stronger crop or reveal better serves the event.

## Camera

- Default to a viewer-subordinate position: distant low camera or human-height camera, level to slightly upward. Do not default to bird's-eye, aerial, high-angle, or top-down framing.
- Prefer 70–100mm from a distant low viewpoint when the image must combine dense palace-city prosperity with a colossal structure looming behind or above it. Let rooflines, terraces, clouds, and the main mass compress into a heavy layered wall of civilization.
- Use 24–32mm rectilinear lenses for bridge approaches, low viewpoints, column fields, gates, and underside reveals. Keep the camera far enough away to preserve a readable hierarchy.
- Use 35–50mm for portals, terraces, interiors, and believable architectural fragments at human height.
- Default to deep focus with controlled verticals. Use atmosphere, overlap, and contrast steps rather than bokeh to separate planes.
- Keep the camera distant enough that wide-angle perspective reveals the spatial hierarchy instead of turning the nearest surface into meaningless distortion.
- Use an elevated overview only when the user explicitly asks to understand the city's geographic layout. Never choose it merely because the scene contains clouds, islands, or many buildings.

For a `繁华压迫` frame, keep the horizon near the lower third, put tiny figures and the viewing threshold along the lower edge, compress dense secondary districts across the middle, and let the cropped principal megastructure dominate the middle and upper frame. The viewer should feel beneath or in front of the structure, never above it.

## High-key restrained color

Create luminosity through value and temperature before increasing saturation:

- Keep about 65–80% of the frame in pale stone, cloud white, muted jade, blue-gray sky, cool water, or other low-to-medium chroma luminous bases.
- Use about 15–25% moderate color in slate blue, mineral green, dark timber, subdued vermilion, or weathered bronze.
- Reserve the strongest warm chroma for about 3–8% of sunlit roof edges, lacquer accents, garments, banners, or metal catches unless the user explicitly asks for more.
- Keep the scene high in brightness with clean color separation. Low chroma must never become muddy gray, beige haze, or dead grading.
- Preserve explicit high-saturation requests by expanding material color selectively while retaining a large neutral sky, cloud, stone, water, or shadow stabilizer.

## Light and atmosphere

- Favor a low sun from the side or rear, commonly near dawn or late afternoon, with warm ivory, pale apricot, or restrained gold highlights.
- Use cool high-altitude skylight as the ambient fill; keep shadows open in cyan-gray, slate blue, blue-green, or violet-gray.
- Let thin localized mist scatter light around thresholds, waterfalls, foundations, and cloud edges. Never apply a uniform fog veil.
- Use soft highlight rolloff and visible material edge response. Avoid blown-out white clouds and black architectural undersides.
- Omit lens flare by default; allow one faint optically motivated flare only when it strengthens the threshold event.

## Material direction

Favor monumental pale stone, weathered jade, carved cliff-like foundations, matte mineral surfaces, restrained lacquer timber, dark bronze, narrow gilded edges, water, and dense volumetric cloud. Reduce ornamental variety on the largest masses; place fine Chinese detail on thresholds, rooflines, doors, column bases, and human-scale surfaces so the structure reads as architecture rather than abstract concrete.

## Prompt order

Assemble as:

`ratio and camera -> inherited measurable scale proofs -> fixed five layers and 40–60% content-bearing air -> scene route -> dominant spatial event -> crop or occlusion -> railing-free human threshold -> monumental Eastern construction and localized detail -> rear-facing immortals and costume grammar -> high-key restrained palette -> low side/backlight -> localized atmospheric scattering -> deep-focus cinematic material rendering -> global and route invariants`

## Audit

Revise if any answer is no:

- Is the focal idea a spatial event rather than a catalog of objects?
- Are all five inherited layers readable with 40–60% content-bearing air?
- Are four or more measurable scale proofs present beyond cropping alone?
- Does at least one structure feel larger than the frame?
- Do threshold, occlusion, and scale markers reveal the world intentionally?
- Are figures small enough to function as evidence rather than protagonists?
- When figures are present, are rear angle, calm action, structured immortal clothing, and one wind direction explicit?
- Is the image luminous without relying on high saturation everywhere?
- Are warm highlights localized and cool shadows transparent?
- Does mist sculpt depth without hiding the architecture?
- Is the result recognizably Eastern without looking like a historical reconstruction, European castle, modern megacity, or sci-fi concrete monolith?
- Does the camera remain subordinate to the megastructure, and was any high-angle overview explicitly requested?
- Are all edges railing-free unless the user explicitly requested railings?

Avoid a fully visible palace centered in empty clouds, default bird's-eye or aerial city views, dense red-and-gold theme-park color, excessive filigree on every giant surface, identical stamped roofs, muddy blue-gray grading, opaque fog, crushed black foregrounds, fisheye distortion, multiple equal wonders, or a tiny picturesque pavilion that lacks megastructure evidence.
