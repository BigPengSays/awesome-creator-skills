# Inhabited celestial realm route

Use this route to design a place where immortals plausibly live at mythic scale. Apply [xianxia-master-rules.md](xianxia-master-rules.md) first; city density and topology may expand but never replace its scale, five layers, breathing air, architecture, railing, figure, or costume rules. The result is not a historical reconstruction and not a lone palace floating above clouds.

## Route promise

Prioritize in this order:

1. **Mythic invention**: make the world impossible in reality through floating continents, kilometer-scale cloud cities, vertical heavens, suspended transit, celestial waterfalls, divine trees, star rivers, or other coherent wonders.
2. **Eastern recognition**: express the civilization through flying eaves, bracket sets, layered roofs, open terraces, courtyards, gates, towers, galleries, stairs, lacquered timber, mineral pigments, bronze, jade, and restrained gilding.
3. **Cinematic credibility**: keep one perspective system, motivated light, physical materials, volumetric clouds, scale comparisons, and readable spatial continuity.

Do not let historical building practice cap the scale or geometry. Use it as vocabulary and ordering logic only.

## Master inheritance

- Map the fixed five layers to: near giant threshold; broad inhabited circulation surface; one ruling district or divine axis; connected subordinate districts; extreme-distance settlement, higher heaven, or terminal sky.
- Preserve 40–60% content-bearing air between, below, behind, and through districts. Use courtyards, streets, portals, cloud gaps, open water, void beneath platforms, and atmospheric separation; do not replace breathing air with a continuous roof carpet.
- Include at least four measurable scale proofs. Use adult-to-column, gate, platform, bridge, tree, cliff, or city-axis comparisons in addition to density and repetition.
- Keep all platforms, bridges, stairs, galleries, terraces, streets, and cloud harbors railing-free unless the user explicitly requests a railing.
- When inhabitants are visible, default them to 1–4% rear-facing or rear-oblique adults with calm actions, structured immortal clothing, and one coherent wind direction. City activity may multiply tiny figures but may not turn them into front-facing portraits or generic silhouettes.
- Keep the largest city structures immense, calm, and structurally legible. Concentrate fine ornament at human-readable thresholds instead of coating every giant surface.

## Ensemble hierarchy

Replace the single-landmark rule with one ruling hierarchy:

- Establish one principal palace district, divine axis, city crown, sacred mountain core, or elevated imperial precinct as the dominant mass.
- Add two to four subordinate districts with lower scale or contrast.
- Use many minor roofs, towers, courtyards, open terraces, bridges, stairs, waterfalls, and cloud harbors as urban texture.
- Connect districts visibly. A settlement must show circulation or infrastructure rather than unrelated monuments scattered in fog.
- Vary roof height, platform elevation, spacing, overlap, and orientation. Avoid identical stamped buildings.

High density is desirable; equal visual dominance is not. Reduce clutter through hierarchy, repetition, and connected spatial logic instead of deleting the city.

## World topologies

Choose one topology that fits the request; do not default repeatedly to the same composition.

- **Axial ascension**: gates, courts, terraces, and palace districts rise toward a high celestial crown.
- **Terraced floating continent**: a vast airborne landmass carries cascading city levels, cliff palaces, waterfalls, and lower cloud docks.
- **Suspended island network**: several inhabited islands connect through long bridges, flying galleries, cloud ferries, and ceremonial causeways.
- **Nine-heavens stack**: multiple inhabited strata float above one another, each smaller, brighter, or more sacred toward the summit.
- **Divine-axis ring city**: districts encircle a colossal tree, pillar, mountain, star instrument, sun wheel, or waterfall shaft without turning it into a Western sci-fi megastructure.
- **Cloud-cliff metropolis**: palaces cling to immense vertical cliffs and continue above, below, and beyond the frame.
- **Horizon palace belt**: a laterally continuous skyline of courts and towers stretches across a cloud ocean, suitable for 16:9 or 21:9 spectacle.

## Ensemble macro gate

For an epic or ultra-macro inhabited realm, require visible evidence:

1. Show the fixed five layers: near giant threshold, human-bearing circulation surface with tiny inhabitants, ruling district, connected subordinate districts, and an extreme-distance settlement or higher heaven.
2. Keep environment-led adults around 1–4% of frame height; use 5–8% only when a full silhouette must read.
3. Let architecture continue beyond a side, top, lower cloud layer, or horizon so the settlement feels larger than the frame.
4. Usually spread the built world across most of the usable width and maintain architecture in near, middle, and far planes. Do not isolate one palace inside an empty cloud field.
5. Use one principal mass, two to four supporting masses, and numerous small architectural repetitions.
6. Include connective evidence such as corridors, bridges, stairs, terraces, cloud roads, ferry platforms, or aqueduct-like waterfalls.
7. Place clouds between, below, and behind districts. Use gaps and illuminated edges so clouds reveal scale rather than erase buildings.
8. Preserve roughly 40–60% content-bearing air. Distribute it through open courts, streets, portal air, water, cloud gaps, void beneath platforms, and separation between vertical districts rather than reserving one empty sky block.

If the frame only shows one palace, one gate, or one platform, route it back to `单体仙境` even when the prompt says `天宫`.

## Camera and clarity

- For `繁华压迫`, favor a distant low or human-height camera with 70–100mm compression so dense palace layers accumulate behind tiny figures and beneath the ruling megastructure.
- Use a distant low 24–35mm rectilinear lens for approaches, bridges, stairs, gates, and underside reveals; use 35–50mm at human height for threshold views and believable architectural proportions.
- For low-angle grandeur, keep the camera low but far enough away to reveal the whole city hierarchy; control vertical convergence and prohibit fisheye distortion.
- Default to deep focus and large depth of field. Keep the near threshold, human-bearing surface, principal district, subordinate districts, and terminal distance readable.
- Use elevated reveals only when the user explicitly selects `城市总览` or asks for bird's-eye geography, district relationships, or island distribution. Do not infer elevation from `大全景`, `云海`, `浮岛`, or `仙城`.
- Use atmosphere through smaller scale, cooler distance, and stepped contrast. Do not create depth by applying a uniform gray fog layer.
- Keep foreground viewing platforms subordinate, commonly around 8–15% of frame area, detached from the edges when requested, and never blocking the principal city mass.

## Prosperity under pressure

Express prosperity without an aerial map:

- Place tiny rear-facing immortals and a human-bearing threshold in the lower frame; give principal scale markers structured robes and one coherent wind direction.
- Fill the middle distance with overlapping roofs, courts, bridges, galleries, cloud traffic, waterfalls, terraces, lights, and processional movement.
- Place one ruling palace, gate, platform, wall, or divine axis behind or above those districts; crop it with the top or side edge so it remains immeasurable.
- Keep visible vertical faces, foundations, columns, and undersides. Roof coverage alone proves density but not pressure.
- Keep the horizon low and let the city continue beyond both the frame and the cloud layers.

## Fantasy density without generic fantasy

Add two to four mutually coherent impossible systems, not a random catalog:

- palaces embedded in floating mountain continents;
- waterfalls falling through several cloud strata;
- cloud stairs and suspended galleries connecting districts;
- a higher heaven or distant divine capital visible beyond the main city;
- enormous celestial trees, bells, star instruments, sun wheels, or jade pillars integrated into the urban plan;
- cloud harbors, crane routes, flying boats, light bridges, or ritual processions that imply habitation;
- inverted peaks, hanging gardens, airborne rivers, or luminous cloud channels governed by the same world logic.

Avoid European castles with Chinese roofs, modern skyscraper grids, sci-fi neon skylines, game-map clutter, or a historical palace merely pasted onto clouds.

## Palace-color realism

Use a `华彩但不过曝` material route when the user wants vivid color:

- Allow vermilion lacquer, mineral green and blue painting, gilded or bronze roof details, white jade, dark wood, colored glaze, and bright sky to remain visibly saturated.
- Separate saturation by material instead of reducing all architecture to accent color.
- Keep clouds, pale stone, and white jade near-neutral; use them as luminous stabilizers.
- Confine warm gold to sunlit edges, roof ridges, metal, and selected lit planes. Do not apply a global orange-gold wash.
- Keep shadows transparent cyan, blue-gray, blue-green, or violet-gray according to the lighting premise.
- Preserve high visibility, clean color separation, and readable distant silhouettes; prohibit muddy gray grading.

## Prompt assembly

Order a `神域聚居地` prompt as:

`ratio -> camera height, distance, tilt, and focal length -> inherited measurable scale proofs -> fixed five layers and 40–60% content-bearing air -> impossible inhabited-world premise -> chosen topology -> ruling and subordinate districts -> railing-free connective infrastructure -> monumental Eastern construction and localized ornament -> tiny rear-facing immortals and costume grammar -> vivid material palette -> one motivated light source -> discrete volumetric clouds and high-visibility atmosphere -> deep-focus cinematic rendering -> global and route invariants`

Group architecture by district and depth rather than stacking a long unstructured list of nouns.

## Route audit

Reject or revise the prompt if any answer is no:

- Does the scene depict a lived-in world rather than one decorative landmark?
- Are all five inherited layers readable with 40–60% content-bearing air?
- Are four or more measurable scale proofs present beyond density alone?
- Do buildings remain present across near, middle, far, and extreme-distance planes?
- Is one principal hierarchy clearly dominant without deleting subordinate districts?
- Are districts visibly connected?
- Does scale come from mass, quantity, height, continuity, and tiny inhabitants together?
- Do clouds reveal the city instead of hiding it?
- Are material colors vivid but separated from the lighting grade?
- Would the city still feel immense if the figures were removed?
- Does the result remain recognizably Eastern without becoming a historical reconstruction?
- Are all edges railing-free unless the user explicitly requested railings?
- When people are present, are rear angle, scale, calm action, immortal costume, and one wind direction explicit?
- If the goal is prosperity and pressure, is the viewer below or at human height rather than above the city?
- Was any elevated overview explicitly requested rather than inferred from the desire to show scale?
